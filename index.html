Multi-factor stock prediction model (version 3.1)

Question
Can combining several kinds of signals forecast a stock’s direction better than any single indicator?
Data
Prices, the VIX, the 10-year Treasury yield, the US dollar index, sector and peer funds, options data, news sentiment and company fundamentals, pulled with the yfinance library.
Method
Two forecast horizons: the next trading day and the next 21 trading days. Three tree-based models (Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM) are stacked, validated with walk-forward splits, and their probabilities are calibrated. ARIMA and LSTM models add price forecasts.
Output
A combined score that turns into a buy or sell signal only when the model’s confidence reaches 60 percent.
Tools
Python, Google Colab, scikit-learn, XGBoost, LightGBM, ARIMA, LSTM

This is a learning project, not investment advice. A backtest shows how a method would have performed in the past, not how it will perform in the future.
